The Applicant (2026)

Logline: While recording an audition for a prestigious international dance program, a high school senior spirals into frustration as he struggles to explain what dance truly means to him.

Description: The Applicant (2026) was produced as part of the University of Connecticut’s DMD 4835: Narrative Short Film Production course. The film is an original story written, produced, and directed by me, and edited by Carly Clarke.

The Challenge - Producer's Business Report (2026)

Description: The Challenge - Producer's Business Report was developed in its entirety as part of the University of Connecticut’s DMD 3840: Creative Producing for Independent Film course. The project centered on a pre-existing screenplay provided for the assignment, following six strangers trapped inside a deadly reality competition for a chance to win ten million dollars. The report explores the film’s creative and commercial development through casting considerations, script coverage, comparable films, financing structures, tax incentives, return-on-investment projections, marketing strategies, and distribution planning.

Halloween (1978) - Scene Recreation (2025)

Description: This scene was produced as part of the University of Connecticut’s DMD 3230: Cinematic Storytelling course, which challenged students to recreate scenes from well-known films while applying techniques associated with its specific genre. This recreation focused on the horror genre through a scene originally directed by John Carpenter for Halloween (1978). My contributions to the project included producing, directing the recreation, and editing the piece, including sound design.

🏆 Operation: Wardrobe (2025)

Logline: When her sister steals her favorite shirt, a college student and her best friend stage an increasingly ridiculous late-night heist to get it back.

Description: Operation: Wardrobe (2025) was produced as part of the 2025 DMCT 48 Hour Film Challenge in partnership with the Norwalk Film Festival and supported by the Connecticut Office of Film, Television & Digital Media. The film received Judges First Place at the 2026 Digital Media Connecticut Awards. My contributions to the project included producing, writing the screenplay, and co-directing alongside my peers Carly Clarke and Austin Kipphut. Learn more about the challenge here: www.digitalmediact.com.

Call Me By Your Name (2017) - Scene Recreation (2025)

Description: This scene was produced as part of the University of Connecticut’s DMD 3230: Cinematic Storytelling course, which challenged students to recreate scenes from well-known films while applying techniques associated with its specific genre. This recreation focused on the romance genre through a scene originally directed by Luca Guadagnino for Call Me By Your Name (2017).
